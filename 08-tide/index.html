The harbour at 4 a.m.

Scroll - the morning follows you

A café that opens with the light.

TIDE sits on the old fish quay. We pour the first coffee when the first boat comes in, and we close when the gulls give up. The menu is whatever the morning landed, written on the window in chalk.

No reservations, no Wi-Fi password, no rush. Twelve stools, one long counter, and a window that does what your scroll just did - every single day, whether anyone is watching or not.
